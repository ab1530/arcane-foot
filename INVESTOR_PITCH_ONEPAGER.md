## THE PROBLEM (€5B Market)

**Football scouting is stuck in the 2000s:**

- **Desktop-only tools** (Wyscout, InStat) = unusable on mobile
- **€300-5000/year pricing** = inaccessible to 95% of scouts
- **Manual workflows** = scouts spend 80% time on admin, 20% on actual scouting
- **Zero automation** = every report typed manually
- **No collaboration** = siloed tools, no team features

**Result**: 10M+ scouts worldwide frustrated, underserved market.
